## What the cafe actually is (from the evidence)

Not a moody "eco-luxe" lounge at all. In post after post, Moh Maya shows up as:

1. **A workshop & community hub.** Recurring creative workshops hosted at the cafe: Paint-Your-Own **Bento Cake** workshops (with a local art host and a home-bakery partner), tumbler-bedazzling workshops, a Hindi writing workshop, art/DIY sessions, and a nail-art pop-up weekend (nail dates + coffee). A photography community celebrated its first anniversary there. People bring cousins, friends and loved ones — groups, not just couples.
2. **A bakery story.** The cafe's own recent post teases something "delicious" coming — a new bakery angle; its bakery partner is a 100%-eggless home bakery launching with the cafe. **Bento cakes** are the signature, most-talked-about item.
3. **A Gen-Z bestie hangout.** Visitor captions cluster around besties/trios, friendship-day outings, "nail dates", birthday-ish celebrations, cafe-hopping reels and "hidden gem of Prayagraj" round-ups. Recurring visitor words: *aesthetic, pinteresty, cozy, peaceful, gorgeous, unmatched vibes*.
4. **Everyday cafe food.** One visitor's wordplay post confirms the staples people associate with it: **pizza, burger, coffee** (and a "caffeine rush"). Coffee is central; cakes/bakes are the differentiator.

## Target crowd

Predominantly young women and friend groups, students and young creators from Prayagraj — people who come for photos, workshops, birthdays and slow hangouts, not for a formal dinner.

## Design language inferred

- Bright, warm, daytime, camera-friendly — the opposite of the previous build's dark wood/stone "eco-luxe" theme.
- Palette direction: cream/ivory base, warm cocoa-brown text, terracotta/amber accents, blush-pink and soft-green highlights (cake, frosting, plants, celebration energy).
- Typography direction: a warm, characterful serif for headlines (Fraunces) + a clean rounded sans (Outfit) — friendly, not luxury-stiff.
- Content direction: lead with **cakes + workshops + bestie adda**, then coffee/food; show community proof (workshops hosted, creator meetups) instead of fake luxury claims.
